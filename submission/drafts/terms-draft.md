## Operator and service

BioCartoon is operated by Joseph Rich. The plugin helps users search a public
library of scientific illustrations, inspect previews and attribution, obtain
available download links, discover shared slide templates, and prepare artwork
contributions for human review. BioCartoon is free to use. Some host features,
such as image generation, may have separate availability or account requirements
set by that host.

For help, use https://github.com/josephrich98/biocartoon-app/issues. For a request
containing private information, use the contact method on the privacy page
instead of posting that information in a public issue.

## Illustration licenses

Each illustration and template keeps its own stated license. Check the individual
record and original source before using an item, and satisfy any attribution,
ShareAlike or other conditions that apply. BioCartoon's software license does not
replace these individual asset licenses. A search result is not a claim that
every catalog item is CC0 or public domain.

Review artwork for scientific accuracy and suitability before using it in a
publication, presentation, teaching resource or other work. Generated or adapted
artwork also needs this review.

## Contributions

Submit artwork only when you may share it under the selected license. Provide
accurate attribution and disclose whether generative AI was used. The supported
contribution licenses are CC0-1.0 and CC-BY-4.0; generative-AI contributions are
accepted only under CC0-1.0. An adaptation must also satisfy the original work's
license and the contribution flow's compatibility checks.

Before a contribution is sent, review the artwork, metadata and license and
explicitly approve the submission. Uploads enter a human review queue. A
submission ID acknowledges receipt; it does not guarantee approval, publication
or designation as official BioCartoon artwork. If accepted and published,
the artwork is shared under its selected license and public attribution.

Do not include confidential information or personal data in artwork or public
metadata. Any optional contributor email is intended for the reviewers and is
handled as described in the privacy policy.

## Hosted limits and permitted use

The hosted plugin currently accepts PNG or SVG submissions up to 2 MB. Other
formats or resizing require preparation outside the hosted endpoint, such as
through the local BioCartoon CLI. The hosted service applies request and
contribution limits; an error or quota response is not confirmation of a
successful submission.

Do not use the service to submit material you lack permission to share, insert
executable or malicious content, bypass moderation or access controls, or disrupt
other users' access. The public plugin does not provide tools to approve its own
contributions, bypass another provider's licenses, or delete a user's cloud projects.

## Privacy and third-party services

The BioCartoon privacy policy is available at
https://www.biocartoon.com/privacy. It must be updated to cover the hosted MCP
data flow before these terms are adopted for the plugin. ChatGPT and any other
host, AI provider or linked third-party service applies its own terms and policies.
