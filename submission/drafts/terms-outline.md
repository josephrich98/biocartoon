# BioCartoon plugin terms — outline for publisher review

No published terms page was found in the website source. This outline is not
terms of service, has not been adopted, and supplies no completed terms URL.
Do not put a guessed /terms address in the submission package.

## Facts available for a draft

- The service searches a public catalog, provides illustration metadata and
  download links, previews shared templates and accepts contributions for review.
- Each illustration keeps its own license and credit requirements. The catalog
  is not uniformly public domain or CC0.
- The software repository has a BSD-2-Clause license; that software license does
  not replace the separate licenses of illustration assets or establish service
  terms for the hosted plugin.
- A contributor chooses CC0-1.0 or CC-BY-4.0 as supported by the contribution flow.
  Generative-AI artwork can be submitted only under CC0-1.0. Adaptations must also
  satisfy the original illustration's license.
- Contributions require explicit confirmation and remain pending until a human
  reviewer decides whether to accept them. Submitting does not guarantee approval,
  an official designation or publication.
- Hosted PNG/SVG submissions have size limits, and the free hosted service has
  request/query quotas. The local CLI has additional conversion capabilities.

## Publisher decisions needed before writing or publishing terms

1. Identify the individual or entity operating the hosted service and the intended
   support/contact address.
2. Confirm any required age/eligibility provisions and how they relate to the
   existing privacy page's account provisions.
3. Define acceptable use, contributor permissions, moderation/removal procedures,
   and treatment of third-party rights complaints.
4. Confirm whether the service involves commerce and, if so, what users purchase
   and the applicable payment/refund arrangements.
5. Decide the provisions for availability, suspension, changes, warranties,
   liability and dispute handling. Do not infer governing law or jurisdiction
   from the maintainer's location.
6. Choose a stable page on a domain the publisher controls. Review the final text,
   publish it, verify that it is accessible without signing in, and only then set
   interface.termsOfServiceURL in submission/listing.json.

The submission's required legal/policy attestations must be completed by the
authorized developer in the portal, not inferred from this outline.
