# Contributing

Contributions are welcome: bug reports, fixes, features, and new templates.

You keep the copyright in your work. Code you contribute is licensed under
the [BSD 2-Clause License](LICENSE), the same license as the rest of the repository. Artwork
(illustrations, icons, templates) stays under the license you choose for it, CC0 or CC BY 4.0;
see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
Credit is shown under whatever name you give, and a pseudonym is fine. If you'd rather not be
credited, submit artwork under CC0 and leave the credit name blank.

## Third-party assets

If a pull request adds code, images, or icons you didn't create, say where they come from and
under what license. Assets must allow redistribution and modification, including commercial
use (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, MIT, BSD, public domain, or similar; no NC or ND licenses), and their
license and credit go in the asset database.
